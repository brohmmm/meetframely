03Template anatomy

It's not a worksheet. It's a script.

Every template tells you what to say.

Facilitator goal

Help the group create one shared, reasonably complete problem statement.

What you say

"I would rather we start with something slightly messy than pretend we already have the perfect framing."

What to watch for
  • Solutions instead of problems.
  • Vague language like "improve the journey".
  • Several problems mixed together.
When it goes sideways
  • Someone jumps to a solution?"Let's park that. What problem would that solution solve?"
